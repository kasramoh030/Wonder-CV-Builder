## Full description — English

> **A CV that fits the country you are sending it to.**
>
> Most CV builders give you a template and wish you luck. Wonder CV Builder
> knows that a CV for Germany, Iran, the UK and the United States is not the
> same document — and it tells you what each market expects while you write.
>
> **Built for real differences between markets**
> • Iran: Jalali dates, military service and driving licence sections
> • Germany: the conventional German structure, photo optional
> • Europe: Europass-style as one option among several
> • United Kingdom: date of birth, marital status, nationality and photo off
>   by default, because they are not expected
> • United States: US Letter, one page as the ideal, resumes and academic CVs
>   structured separately
> • International: a neutral base when you are applying in several places
>
> **It works with no internet at all**
> Writing, analysing, importing and exporting your CV all happen on your
> phone. No account, no sign-in, no upload, no waiting.
>
> **A checker that tells you why**
> The offline analyser scores your CV and shows its evidence: completeness,
> structure, ATS-readiness, language, and — when you paste a job advert — how
> well your CV answers it. Every finding is a problem plus a fix, with a
> priority, so you know what to do first. The score is an estimate from rules,
> not a promise of an interview, and the app says so.
>
> **Real PDFs**
> A4 by default, US Letter for the US, multi-page with proper page breaks,
> selectable text that a recruiter can search, embedded fonts including full
> Persian, and clickable links. Print it, share it, or save it.
>
> **Twelve templates, including a genuinely ATS-safe one**
> The ATS template is single-column, standard headings, no photo, no graphics
> and no tables — because that is what an applicant tracking system can
> actually read. The rest are for people who will be read by a human.
>
> **Your data stays yours**
> No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. No account. Your CVs are stored
> on your device and are never uploaded. Export a JSON backup whenever you
> like, and delete everything with one button.
>
> **Three languages**
> English, Persian (with right-to-left layout) and German, with Gregorian and
> Jalali dates.
>
> **Import an existing CV**
> Bring in a PDF, Word (.docx) or text file. You review everything that was
> read before it becomes a document — never after.
>
> Free to use. No watermark on your PDF. No sign-up screen between you and
> your CV.

## FAQ (for the listing and support replies)

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no sign-up, no login and no cloud. Your CVs live on your device.

**Is my CV uploaded anywhere?**
No. There is no server belonging to us. The only outbound traffic is a
hostname lookup to check whether your device is online, which contains none of
your data.

**Does it work without internet?**
Completely. Building, analysing, importing, exporting and printing all work in
airplane mode.

**Can I open my CV on another device?**
Not automatically — that is the price of not having a cloud. Export the JSON
backup and import it on the other device, or export a PDF to share.

**What happens if I lose my phone?**
Android may include the app's data in your own device backup, which you
control in Android settings. We recommend exporting a JSON backup after any
significant edit.

**Is the score a promise I will get the job?**
No, and no app can promise that. The score measures your document against
conventions and rules; the disclaimer is in the app.

**Why is a photo off by default?**
Because in the United Kingdom and the United States a photo is not expected on
a CV, and in Germany it is optional rather than required. You can switch it on
for any market, and the app will tell you what the local convention is.

**Which file should I send — PDF or Word?**
PDF, from this app. It keeps the layout you designed and its text stays
searchable, which is what both a recruiter and a screening system need.

**Is there a premium version?**
Not in this release. Everything here is free, with no watermark and no limits.
